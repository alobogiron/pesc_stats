                                                        1. 2016-2017. Opinion Mining and sentiment analysis
                                                          Descrição: Descrição: Online social networks are so popular that they have become an important component for people's social interaction. These environments are equally rich in opinionated and emotional texts where users can share their emotions, feelings, ideas and thoughts. Opinion papers are giving more insight into document categorization. These documents help not only users make decisions based on emotional preferences, but also because it contains a true "mine" of data from which it is possible to extract valuable opinions for e-commerce companies, the political field, among others. Thus, due to the increasing volume of opinionated textual information available on the web, there is a great demand for Natural Language Processing (PLN). The ambiguity, co-referencing, the use of slang, grammatical errors, regional dialects, figurative language (such as irony, sarcasm, metaphor, etc.) represent a challenge not only for PLN systems, but also for understanding the meaning for being human. This project comprises a set of activities aimed at scientific research with the aim of extracting emotions from shared texts in the social network Facebook, in the format of comments written in the Portuguese language. Research contributes to an important ongoing topic, that is, the modeling of feeling applied to the detection of emotion. Two models for the representation of emotions will be evaluated in this study. An important contribution of the research is the practical application of emotion models using a lexical approach and in machine learning. This allows a wider exploration of the lexical resources used to detect emotion. The goal is not only to determine specific emotions, but also to show whether the text contains emotions or not. It should be noted that most of the resources for the analysis of texts found in the literature make use of textual bases (corpus) in the English language.. Situação: Concluído; Natureza: Pesquisa.
